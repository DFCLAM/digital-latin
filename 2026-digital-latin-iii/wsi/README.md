# WSI - Word-sense induction

A tentative approach to WSI for mid-latin texts. There are two goals:

1. given a *lemma* and some contexts for which a certain sense of the *lemma* is well-known, getting other contexts bearing the same sense;
2. more generally, trying to guess word senses in context.

A context is intended here as a text fragment of a text of the corpus where a lemma is attested.
